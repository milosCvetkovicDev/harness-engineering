# Write-ups

Rules for public posts. One of the course goals is public write-ups, and the cheapest proof of
understanding is a short post with a number in it. Drafts live in `private/writeups/`. A post comes
into this folder only when you decide to publish it: once the course is published, every push here is
public. That decision never covers material derived from your set-up, client material or source
material.

- One file per post: `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`.
- Each module's "Ship" section suggests a prompt. A good post states one question, shows the
  experiment, gives the result with its sample size, and says what it does not prove.
- Nothing from `private/` goes into a post without a second look.
- No post holds paths, client names or keys, wherever its material comes from.
- Publishing is always your decision. Claude does not post anything.
